NSBM Finder

The NSBM Finder is a Flutter-based mobile application designed to enhance campus life at NSBM Green University by simplifying navigation, communication, and resource management for students and lecturers. It allows students to easily locate lecturer cabins, schedule appointments, chat in real time, reserve study rooms, and receive important notifications, while lecturers can manage bookings, update availability, and communicate directly with students. Powered by Flutter for the frontend and Firebase for authentication, Firestore database, cloud functions, and push notifications, the app also integrates Google Maps and calendar support to provide a seamless and user-friendly campus experience.

Key Features

For Students:

• Search for lecturers by name or department

• View their Google Maps cabin location

• Book appointments to meet lecturers

• Book study rooms directly through the app

• Chat using predefined message templates

• Get real-time notifications for responses

For Lecturers:

• Toggle status as “In Cabin” or “Out of Cabin”

• View, approve, or decline appointment requests

• Chat with students

• Receive push notifications for appointments and messages
